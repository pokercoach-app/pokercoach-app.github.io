Poker Coach

GTO & ICM trainer for iPhone

Poker Coach drills the decisions that end tournaments: all-in or fold from 6 to 30 big blinds, the raise that comes back at you, and bubble spots. Every answer comes from a solved table or a sourced one, named as such, and everything you do stays on your phone.

Support

Questions, a wrong answer you want to report, or anything that did not fit the screen: pokercoach.contact@gmail.com. Please include the hand, the seat and the stack when you report an answer.
