Poker Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

The short version

Everything you do in Poker Coach stays on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics: we do not collect, receive or store any data about you. The one feature that sends anything anywhere is the optional AI coach, it is off until you switch it on, and nothing you send through it is ever used to train AI.

What the app is for

Poker Coach is a study tool. Its training and analysis are for use outside playing sessions. Do not use these tools while an online poker client is open. Follow your venue's rules on devices and note-taking. The About screen says the same thing in the same words.

What the app stores, and where

Your drill answers and progress, the lessons you have finished, your practice days and the milestones you have reached (with their dates), hands you record, notes you keep on opponents, and your settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us — we have no server to send them to. Like any app data, they may be included in the device backups you control through iOS (iCloud or computer backups). You can also save all of it as a single file from the app's Settings or About screen and load that file on another phone; opponent notes and recorded hands go into the file only if you choose to include them. The file is stored wherever you put it and is not sent to us. Your permission for the coach and your API key are never written into it. Deleting the app deletes its storage.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no crash reporting, no account. We never see your hands, your notes, your progress or your questions.

The AI coach (optional, off by default)

The coach reviews hands you recorded, after your session — it is not for a hand in progress. It is powered by Claude, a model run by Anthropic, and it works only after two explicit steps: you allow sending in the app, and you add your own Anthropic API key (stored in the device Keychain, never synced, used only to talk to Anthropic directly from your phone).

When you ask the coach about a hand, the app sends Anthropic: that hand (cards, seats, stacks, actions, plus any note you typed on it), the figures the app computed about it, your question, and a few measured lines about your recent form. When you use "Tidy this note", the app sends the text of that one note and nothing else. Nothing is sent until you ask, and each question is sent only when you ask it.

What is never sent: your opponent notebook (names, venues, reads), your other hands, and anything you did not ask about. The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone; it sends nothing anywhere. Where a read of yours changed a hand's answer, the coach is told that it did, not which read or which player. If you typed a person's name into a note or a question, it travels with your text — the app cannot reliably strip what you wrote, so the safest habit is not to put real full names into free text you plan to send.

Your data is not used to train AI. We run no server and receive nothing. Anthropic's commercial terms prohibit it from training on API content, and Anthropic states API inputs are deleted within about 30 days unless flagged for misuse. Anthropic processes what you send under its own terms and privacy policy, which apply to your API account: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms and https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

You can turn sending off at any time in the app's Settings or About screen. The rest of the app works identically either way.

Notes about other people

The opponent notebook exists so you can keep your own reads on players you face. Those notes stay on your device. They are yours, and keeping personal notes for your own use is your own household activity; publishing or sharing information about identifiable people is your responsibility. The app never uploads the notebook, and "Tidy this note" sends only the note text you explicitly choose to send, never the name field.

Children

The app is a poker training tool and is not directed at children. Its store age rating reflects simulated gambling content.
